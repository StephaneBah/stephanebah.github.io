## PROJECTS & RESEARCH

### [Can Movement Dynamics Classify and Predict Social Behaviours in Mice?](https://airtable.com/app1MtChyjyKEDzAt/shrR68OdIIny48Ynt/tblvFwAE4oqf3M10o/viw3Ma2non57ofw6z/recvccyLjFuCVOfvf)
*Behaviours and Dynamics*
- Analysed the CalMS21 dataset of resident-intruder mouse interactions using 2D pose keypoints and behavioural annotations to study social behaviours including investigation and attack.
- Developed an engagement/confrontation metric from movement velocity and directional alignment to quantify the interaction dynamics between two mice.
- Investigated the ability of temporal and machine learning models to classify and predict social behaviours from movement dynamics.

### [Whisper Small Fine-tuning for African French Accent Recognition](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1rYmkjZBzs8K_cUYDwuKOg2ruTZbejx0x)
- Adapted the Whisper Small model for African French accents using radiology reports. Achieved a Word Error Rate (WER) reduction from 109.6% to 20%, representing a 75% improvement in transcription accuracy, on our test sample.
- Creation of the [first open-source dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/StephaneBah/Africa_Radiology_FR) of African French intended for speech recognition technologies in the medical field.
